Signal chain — A/B the suspects

The stock chain is master → compressor → destination, and master.gain is vol × (0.16 + eff×0.2 + rpm/3600×0.1) — a ceiling of 0.46×vol before the compressor takes another bite. Trim is a post-compressor gain that does not exist in index.html; it's here to find the multiplier that actually sounds right.
